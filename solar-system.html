Celestial Models

Sun

The star at the center of the solar system and the main source of light and energy. Age: 4.6 billion years.

Mercury

The smallest and fastest planet, with a heavily cratered surface. Orbital period: 88 Earth days.

Venus

A dense, hot world wrapped in thick clouds and intense greenhouse conditions. Orbital period: 225 Earth days.

Earth

The third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. Orbital period: 365.25 Earth days.

Mars

The red planet, known for dusty plains, giant volcanoes, and polar ice caps. Orbital period: 687 Earth days.

Jupiter

The largest planet, a gas giant with powerful storms and many moons. Orbital period: 11.86 Earth years.

Saturn

Known for its bright rings and wide system of moons. Orbital period: 29.46 Earth years.

Uranus

An ice giant with a blue-green color and a tilted rotation axis. Orbital period: 84 Earth years.

Neptune

A cold blue ice giant with strong winds and storms. Orbital period: 164.8 Earth years.

Pluto (Dwarf)

A small icy dwarf planet in the outer solar system. Orbital period: 248 Earth years.
